# GenCorpus

A free field guide to Indian transactional law: a ten-stage deal map, fourteen deal types, a clause lab with redlines, working tools, the 2026 regulatory radar, a deal tape and a library.

Live site: https://adityakushagra.github.io/gencorpus/

Education only. Nothing here is legal advice, an offer of legal services, or a solicitation for any advocate or firm. Clause texts are teaching samples, not precedents.

Static site, no build step. Open `index.html`.
